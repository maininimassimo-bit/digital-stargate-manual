# SOP - Enter Safe Mode

| Campo | Valore |
|---|---|
| SOP | Enter Safe Mode |
| Capability | `CAP-SAF-001` |
| Stato | Baseline |

## Purpose

Define the governed procedure for entering a protective operational posture when Safety determines Unsafe, Emergency, Unknown or Disabled conditions.

## Preconditions

- Safety Assessment indicates Unsafe, Emergency, Unknown, Disabled or unrecoverable Warning.
- A schedule, session or observatory operation may be impacted.

## Procedure

| Step | Action | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm current Safety State and reason. | Safety Assessment. |
| 2 | Determine affected schedule/session/equipment context. | Impact reference. |
| 3 | Produce Suspend Observation, Abort Observation, Close Roof Request or Safe Mode output as applicable. | Safety Decision. |
| 4 | Hand off to OSM or Operations for governed execution. | Handoff record. |
| 5 | Record Safety Event and Audit Event. | Event/audit record. |
| 6 | Continue monitoring until Recovery is possible or operations remain held. | Monitoring/recovery reference. |

## Controls

- This SOP does not implement physical shutdown or roof control.
- Safe Mode is a conceptual protective posture.
- Emergency conditions take priority over observation continuity.

## Outputs

- Safe Mode output.
- Safety Event.
- Audit Event.
- Handoff to OSM/Operations.
