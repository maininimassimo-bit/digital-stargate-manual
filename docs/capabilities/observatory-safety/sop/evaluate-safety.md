# SOP - Evaluate Safety

| Campo | Valore |
|---|---|
| SOP | Evaluate Safety |
| Capability | `CAP-SAF-001` |
| Stato | Baseline |

## Purpose

Define the repeatable conceptual procedure for evaluating observatory safety before or during observation activity.

## Preconditions

- Current safety inputs are available or explicitly unknown.
- Related schedule/session context is known when applicable.

## Procedure

| Step | Action | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify context: schedule approval, session start, active session, recovery, maintenance or emergency. | Context reference. |
| 2 | Collect inputs from Weather, OSM, Equipment, Scheduling, Target, Operator, Maintenance, Power, Roof and Communications where applicable. | Input set. |
| 3 | Mark missing or stale inputs as Unknown. | Input status. |
| 4 | Evaluate inputs against governed Safety Policy and Safety Rules. | Rule evaluation. |
| 5 | Determine Safety State. | Safety Assessment. |
| 6 | Produce appropriate safety output. | Safety Decision. |
| 7 | Record Audit Event. | Audit Record. |

## Controls

- Unknown or Disabled shall not authorize unattended operation.
- Safety decision must be traceable to input set and rule evaluation.
- Hardware action is not performed by this SOP.

## Outputs

- Safety Assessment.
- Safety State.
- Safety Decision.
- Audit Event.
