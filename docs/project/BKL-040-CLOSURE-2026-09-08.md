## 4. Accepted architecture outcome

The accepted BKL-040 baseline provides a bounded historical Night Timeline / Observatory Replay projection that:

- preserves repository-proven source identity and source authority;
- normalizes eligible event time onto a canonical UTC axis without substituting file, ingestion, commit, persistence or session-boundary timestamps;
- keeps `UNPLACED` evidence structurally distinct and never invents missing event time;
- synchronizes a bounded set of N.I.N.A., PHD2 and CloudWatcher historical evidence with deterministic ordering;
- calculates exact temporal deltas without introducing skew/coincidence thresholds;
- preserves Citation/Provenance lineage through event, correlation and consumer layers;
- exposes only `READ_ONLY` / `VISUAL_ONLY` consumer semantics;
- rejects synthetic conflict/UNPLACED evidence that is not present in the accepted upstream projection.

## 5. Permanent boundaries

BKL-040 does **not** authorize:

- execution or re-execution of historical commands;
- roof, mount, camera, power or network control;
- automatic remediation or device orchestration;
- present-time Safety inference from historical evidence;
- modification, delegation or bypass of the local Safety Authority or physical interlocks;
- invented clock-skew/coincidence thresholds;
- fabricated Power, Network, Safety, SQM or EAGLE historical channels when an exact governed source contract is absent;
- runtime EAGLE changes or background replay services.

These remain separate future governance decisions.
