## 3. Permanent architecture properties

BKL-044 establishes the following permanent rules for downstream capabilities:

1. Observation, Evidence, Claim, Inference, Recommendation, Conflict and Unknown remain semantically distinct.
2. Validated knowledge fails closed when mandatory evidence, Citation, Provenance or method metadata is missing.
3. Citation and Provenance identities are versioned and traceable.
4. Confidence remains bound to a stable/versioned confidence contract.
5. Consumer/read models remain projections and cannot increase authority, lifecycle or semantic certainty.
6. `observed_at_utc`, `produced_at_utc`, `method_id`, `evidence_refs`, `conflict_refs`, Citation, Provenance and Confidence must be preserved when applicable.
7. AI-derived content remains explicitly marked and advisory/read-only.
8. Missing, conflicting and incomplete evidence remains explicit; it is never silently repaired or promoted.

## 4. Boundaries preserved

BKL-044 does not authorize persistent graph/vector technology, RAG, AI provider/inference runtime, autonomous remediation, observatory command paths, EAGLE runtime changes or Safety Authority coupling. Those choices require separately governed packages.
