## 1. Closure statement

BKL-045 is closed as an accepted, bounded PixInsight workflow-provenance capability. The package delivers governed provenance semantics, a real PixInsight-side evidence probe, deterministic sidecar validation, repository-side reconciliation through the existing AP14-W06 boundary, and a read-only consumer model.

The package does **not** claim complete automatic extraction of PixInsight processing history. The real F3-B OAT demonstrated a fail-closed runtime result with `completeness=UNAVAILABLE`, zero automatically observed processing steps, explicit limitations, and repeatable semantic output. This limitation is retained as an accepted architectural boundary rather than reconstructed or inferred away.

## 2. Accepted increments

- **F2 — Extension mechanism decision:** ADR-008 selected the hybrid evidence-capture + governed exporter strategy.

- **F5 — Read-only consumer/acceptance:** accepted read-only consumer preserving `OBSERVED`/`DECLARED`, `UNAVAILABLE/PARTIAL`, source lineage and no authority escalation.

## 4. Authority and safety boundary

- AP-013 remains authoritative for scientific asset identity, checksum, lifecycle and accepted provenance relationships.
- AP-014/AP14-W06 remains the synchronization/catalog reconciliation boundary.
- PixInsight exports processing evidence only; it does not grant scientific asset/session acceptance.
- `actionAuthority=NONE` remains mandatory.
- Consumers are read-only and expose `acceptanceAuthority=false`.
- No observatory device-control path, Safety Authority coupling, autonomous image processing or AI execution authority is introduced.

## 5. Accepted limitation

Complete ordered PixInsight process-history extraction has **not** been demonstrated through the governed PJSR pilot. Therefore:

- `UNAVAILABLE` and `PARTIAL` remain first-class consumer states;
- missing history must not be inferred, reconstructed or presented as `OBSERVED`;
- user-entered workflow steps remain `DECLARED`;
- any future native-history adapter or richer extraction mechanism requires new runtime evidence and architecture review under ADR-008.

This limitation does not block closure because the package acceptance criterion is fail-closed, evidence-grounded provenance handling, not fabricated completeness.

## 6. Dependency reconciliation and successor

With BKL-045 accepted, the declared dependency blocking **BKL-037 — Session Comparison & Benchmarking** is satisfied. BKL-037 depends on BKL-029, BKL-035 and BKL-045; all three are now accepted repository baselines.

BKL-037 is therefore the next dependency-ready package and may be promoted from `Planned` to `Ready/current` during this closure reconciliation.

## 7. Closure decision

**BKL-045 — CLOSED / ACCEPTED.**

The capability is suitable for downstream read-only consumers and benchmarking only within the preserved provenance-completeness semantics. Downstream packages must not interpret absence of PixInsight process history as evidence that no processing occurred.
