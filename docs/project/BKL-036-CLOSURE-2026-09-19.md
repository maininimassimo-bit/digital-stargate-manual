## Decision

BKL-036 is closed as a bounded, read-only repository capability. The accepted capability preserves the seven-domain telemetry contract, evidence envelope, archived-evidence score projection and deterministic repository snapshot archive.

The current F3 projection remains `UNAVAILABLE`: the repository does not contain current, comparable, present evidence for all mandatory domains. No partial or synthetic score is promoted.

## Boundaries retained

This closure does not authorize or claim:

- live EAGLE30154, CloudWatcher, N.I.N.A. or apparatus transport;
- producer/runtime activation, credentials, retention policy or publication cadence;
- operational interpretation of telemetry;
- score promotion, thresholds, readiness, go/no-go or scheduling;
- remediation, device commands or automatic target selection;
- modification of local physical interlocks or Safety Authority.

BKL-032 remains the authority for readiness/go-no-go decision support. Local physical interlocks remain the only Safety Authority. Any live source/transport or runtime consumer requires a separate governed gate and evidence.
