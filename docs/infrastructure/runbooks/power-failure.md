# Runbook — Power Failure

## Trigger

UPS alarm, mains power loss or unstable power condition.

## Procedure

1. confirm the power event and estimated UPS autonomy;
2. suspend new observations;
3. initiate controlled shutdown;
4. park the mount;
5. close the dome or roof;
6. stop device services;
7. shut down compute nodes if power is not restored;
8. verify safe state after power returns.

Do not resume unattended observation until power stability and safety controls have been validated.
