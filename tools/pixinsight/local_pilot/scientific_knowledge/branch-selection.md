# Scientific reasoning for bounded sibling experiments

This protocol supplies hypotheses and comparison criteria, not facts about the current image, a universal recipe, acceptance thresholds, or additional executable capabilities. The coordinator chooses among operations actually admitted by its installed tool contract. The selected recipe, declared instrument profile, native measurements and provenance determine which hypotheses are relevant. State limitations explicitly. Do not execute arbitrary scripts or assume that a cited process exists locally.

## Define an experiment before choosing its outcome

Identify the exact parent, current domain, geometric grid, treatments already applied, affected tracks, intended benefit and potential harm. Each candidate must derive directly from that parent; its unmodified parent is the control. The control, candidates, rejected outputs, parameters, History and measurements must remain available. Compare the same regions and, for quantitative stellar changes, matched identities with a consistent fitting method. Newly detected populations, different pixel scales, different noise supports or unrelated display stretches cannot establish an improvement.

Choose a small number of informative alternatives within the confirmed bounds. Test a moderate hypothesis first; do not consume the entire trial budget searching for progressively stronger settings. A previous successful result on another target is a hypothesis, not evidence that its parameters fit these masters. Omission is an admissible choice whenever a process is unnecessary or its prerequisites or benefits are not established. If no candidate is supported, preserve the control or stop. Explain which observations would be needed to resolve uncertainty.

## Photometric calibration

For linear LRGB/OSC RGB, require a solution on the exact RGB grid, the declared filter response, detector QE or an explicit substitute, Gaia spectra, reference white and documented background handling. Broad RGB filters and narrowband filters are not interchangeable. Nominal ZWO curves do not prove the response of a particular physical revision. An ideal QE is an approximation, not a measured QHY695A response. For M27, Optolong L-Pro is only the luminance filter: do not use it as an RGB filter.

Inspect native fit evidence: fitted sources, rejected points, gains, residual distributions, chromatic/systematic trends and the actual fit products. Account for saturation, blends, unsuitable sources, gradients and low source count. Execution completion and a visually pleasing RGB do not validate a photometric fit. Preserve a calibrated linear checkpoint. Later saturation, contrast and L-Pro LRGB fusion are presentation changes, not renewed photometric calibration. STOP when fit evidence or an essential instrumental prerequisite is absent.

## Optical correction and deconvolution

Use a suitable linear parent before denoise, stretch or star separation. Assess PSF shape, spatial variation, isolated unsaturated stars, structure width and noise. Appropriate alternatives include the control, correction only, and moderate sharpening. Correction only can address distorted stars while preserving detail strength; sharpening is warranted only when morphology and representative faint structures support its benefit. Do not stack two deconvolutions on one branch. A correction-only trial and a sharpening trial should be siblings unless a separately documented experiment requires their sequence.

Check star cores and wings, anisotropy across the field, negative rims, halos, ringing, artificial fine texture, faint filament discontinuities and changes in photometry. FWHM reduction is descriptive, not proof of new physical resolution. Do not choose simply by the lowest FWHM or eccentricity. State that model-based restoration is an inference constrained by the acquired data. A drizzle grid does not independently prove the sampling supports stronger restoration; changing sampling requires an admitted experiment and common angular comparisons.

## Noise reduction

Start candidates from the same selected linear parent. Compare the control and moderate native denoise settings. If multiple denoisers are available, their installed versions/models and separate adapters must be verified before comparison. Low apparent MRS noise after denoise is not increased photon SNR: transformed and correlated noise changes the estimator's assumptions and support.

Inspect weak nebular structure, small stars, chromatic mottling, texture uniformity, halos, waxy patches and loss of faint filaments in common full-field and 100-percent crops. Noise estimates are contextual diagnostics, not a single objective to minimize. Prefer omission when the image already supports the intended stretch without objectionable noise or when a treatment loses structure. Do not use a different display curve to hide noise in the candidate.

## Stretch

Use the selected linear parent, preserve the calibrated RGB channel ratios with linked transformations where appropriate, and identify which background regions are actually suitable. Try restrained alternatives based on measured distributions, bright-core headroom and faint structure. A target background is a presentation setting, not a physical sky brightness. Never assume that a corner is empty sky.

Check finite pixels, newly clipped shadows/highlights, saturated cores, chromatic changes, star prominence, faint structure and contrast in the central object and peripheral field. Linear/nonlinear variance, MRS estimates and medians are not directly comparable as physical improvement metrics. Display comparisons across a domain transition must disclose that transition. Retain the linear parent and all stretch candidates.

## Fusion and presentation

For LRGB, combine the selected stretched L and RGB with a recorded native operation and inspect color dilution, chromatic halos, alignment and differing star profiles. L-Pro luminance has a different spectral response; apparent detail enhancement does not certify color fidelity. Retain the pre-fusion RGB and L and distinguish their provenance from the fused output.

Compare the unmodified fused control with restrained saturation alternatives on the same nonlinear parent. Vividezza is a presentation preference: stronger saturation must not justify channel clipping, background chromatic noise, oversaturated stars or false color claims. Omit local contrast or star separation if a verified mask/adapter and a specific justified benefit are absent. Mask presence alone does not prove its quality.

## Selection, stopping and reporting

The coordinator should state the hypothesis, measured/visible benefits, detected harms, comparison limitations and why the selected checkpoint is preferred to the control and other tested siblings. If paired stellar fits, background validity, independent registration accuracy or quantitative uncertainty are unavailable, explicitly say so and avoid conclusions requiring them. Representative visual checks and integrity can support a bounded presentation choice; they do not constitute complete scientific validation.

Do not finish merely because the final file exists or the budget is exhausted. Missing required calibration, invalid geometry, incomplete receipts, altered originals, ambiguous provider consumption, unverified native completion or unsupported operations require a preserved stop. Final completion means technical readiness for private Owner review. Acceptance, publication and milestone closure remain separate events.
